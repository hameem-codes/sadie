## Clinical Synthesis of Follow-up & Outcome Evidence
1. **Long-Term Outcome Principles:** The author explicitly states that *'lack of complications does not necessarily mean a good outcome'*, advocating for rigorous long-term quality control.
2. **Biological Healing Timelines:** Bone consolidates sufficiently for functional loads (chewing) by ~1 month, soft diet is maintained for 4–6 weeks, and scars and nerve injuries require 18+ months to mature.
3. **Late-Onset Complications:** Enophthalmos becomes noticeable by ~3 months; condylar resorption, titanium particle migration, dental devitalization, and frontal sinus mucoceles take years or lifetimes to become clinically manifest.
4. **Occlusal & Functional Assessment:** Occlusion cannot be judged simply by asking the patient to bite together; dynamic group function, patient perception, and active mandibular excursion (forward and lateral) are required.
