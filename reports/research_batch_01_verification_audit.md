# Research Evidence Batch 01 Verification & Audit Report

## Maxillofacial Fracture Treatment-Outcome Evaluator Project — Phase 3

### 1. Executive Summary & Quality Control Scope
This report presents the rigorous verification audit of **14 primary research publications** (`PAP-001` to `PAP-014`) and their corresponding **14 extracted research evidence records** (`RES-EVD-001` to `RES-EVD-014`).

In accordance with project quality standards:
1. **Zero Hallucination / Inference:** Findings were verified directly against verifiable bibliographic records, PubMed metadata, and accessible primary sources. No full-text findings were inferred from abstracts alone.
2. **Standardized Verification Statuses Assigned:**
   - `VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE`: 3 records (100% full text verified via open-access PubMed Central / PMC).
   - `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`: 11 records (verified through PubMed/MEDLINE and publisher metadata; full text paywalled/restricted).
   - `BIBLIOGRAPHY_VERIFIED_CLAIM_UNVERIFIED`: 0 records.
   - `NOT_VERIFIED`: 0 records.
   - `CONTRADICTED_OR_INCORRECT`: 0 records.
3. **Bibliographic Correction:** The author list for `PAP-005` (Bly et al. 2013, *JAMA Facial Plast Surg*) was audited and updated to its exact published citation: *Randall A. Bly, Shu-Hong Chang, Maria Cudejkova, Jack J. Liu, Kris S. Moe*.
4. **Boundary & Threshold Rules:** All ungrounded numerical cutoffs were rejected or flagged as `NO EXPLICIT THRESHOLD REPORTED`.

### 3. Granular Evidence Record Audit (Task 2)

#### Record RES-EVD-001 (Ho et al. 2016)
- **Outcome Domain:** Skeletal alignment & facial symmetry
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Feasibility of 3D virtual mirroring across mid-sagittal plane and surface matching using ICP algorithms.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Validates 3D surface registration methodology using 26 healthy control CTs. Does **not** establish a surgical reduction threshold or clinical success score.
- **Boundary Restriction:** `NO EXPLICIT CLINICAL SUCCESS THRESHOLD REPORTED`.

#### Record RES-EVD-002 (Ho et al. 2017)
- **Outcome Domain:** Normative midfacial symmetry
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Baseline physiological asymmetry of the zygomaticomaxillary complex in uninjured adults.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that in 15 uninjured trauma controls, $\text{MSD} = 0.14 \pm 0.12\text{ mm}$ (range $0.002\text{--}0.881\text{ mm}$) and $\text{RMSD} = 0.87 \pm 0.21\text{ mm}$ (range $0.56\text{--}1.98\text{ mm}$).
- **Boundary Restriction:** Represents physiological variation in a small Dutch cohort ($n=15$); cannot be extrapolated as a universal post-reduction success threshold.

#### Record RES-EVD-003 (Whitehouse et al. 1994)
- **Outcome Domain:** Orbital volume & enophthalmos
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE` (PMC504888)
- **Claim Verified:** Linear regression ratio between post-traumatic CT orbital volume expansion and enophthalmos.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified in full text that each $1.0\text{ cm}^3$ ($1.0\text{ mL}$) increase in bony orbital volume results in $0.8\text{ mm}$ of late enophthalmos ($r=0.79, P<0.001$).
- **Boundary Restriction:** Derives a mathematical predictive ratio, not a surgical passing score. Conflating this ratio with a binary success threshold is methodologically invalid.

#### Record RES-EVD-004 (Zhang et al. 2012)
- **Outcome Domain:** Orbital herniation & enophthalmos
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Anatomical site-specific correlation of herniated orbital tissue with secondary enophthalmos.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that in 23 secondary enophthalmos cases (mean $4.0\text{ mm}$), herniated soft tissue **posterior to the eyeball equator** significantly drove globe recession ($P<.05$).
- **Boundary Restriction:** `NO EXPLICIT THRESHOLD REPORTED`. Study identifies anatomical location as the primary driver, but does not provide an acceptable residual volume cutoff.

#### Record RES-EVD-005 (Bly et al. 2013)
- **Outcome Domain:** Orbital reconstruction accuracy
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Volumetric restoration accuracy comparing computer guidance to freehand repair.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that computer guidance achieved mean volume difference of $0.74\text{ mL}$ vs $2.12\text{ mL}$ conventional ($P=.003$), lowering enophthalmos rates ($9\%$ vs $38\%$). Author list corrected.
- **Boundary Restriction:** Achieving $<1.0\text{ mL}$ was an empirical observation of computer-guided precision, not a validated binary cutoff separating surgical success from failure.

#### Record RES-EVD-006 (Eckelt et al. 2006)
- **Outcome Domain:** Mandibular functional recovery
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Superior objective functional recovery of ORIF over closed treatment in condylar process fractures.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that operative treatment produced significantly greater mean MIO ($46.5\text{ mm}$ vs $40.9\text{ mm}$, $P=0.001$), protrusion ($7.3\text{ mm}$ vs $4.7\text{ mm}$), and fewer lateral shifts ($19\%$ vs $66\%$).
- **Boundary Restriction:** Both groups achieved mean openings $>40\text{ mm}$; study proves relative surgical superiority, not an absolute binary passing threshold of $40\text{ mm}$.

#### Record RES-EVD-007 (Ellis et al. 2000)
- **Outcome Domain:** Occlusal restoration & malocclusion
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Postoperative malocclusion incidence in closed vs open condylar fracture management.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified in 137 patients that closed treatment produced a significantly higher percentage of malocclusion across all initial ramus shortening categories.
- **Boundary Restriction:** `NO EXPLICIT QUANTITATIVE MILLIMETER THRESHOLD REPORTED`. Evaluated categorically (malocclusion present vs absent), not as a millimeter step-off metric.

#### Record RES-EVD-008 (Adeyemi et al. 2012)
- **Outcome Domain:** IMF duration & bone healing
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Healing outcomes, MIO recovery, and oral hygiene comparing 2 weeks vs 4–6 weeks of IMF.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that 2 weeks of IMF achieved satisfactory bone healing without non-union, with earlier recovery of mouth opening, less weight loss, and better hygiene.
- **Boundary Restriction:** Restricted to minimally displaced fractures in the tooth-bearing area in compliant young adults; cannot be generalized to comminuted or panfacial fractures.

#### Record RES-EVD-009 (Kroon et al. 1991)
- **Outcome Domain:** Mandibular osteosynthesis stability
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** In vitro biomechanical instability of single miniplate fixation under functional loads.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that single upper border miniplates fail to neutralize inferior border distraction and torsional torque during simulated biting.
- **Boundary Restriction:** Biomechanical in vitro model only; does not provide clinical union rates, infection statistics, or patient functional scores.

#### Record RES-EVD-010 (Nagase et al. 2005)
- **Outcome Domain:** Long-term hardware complications
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Long-term plate removal incidence ($33.3\%$) and complication drivers over a 13-year period.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that $64.4\%$ of removals were complication-driven, with hardware palpability, cold intolerance, and pain accounting for $72.4\%$ of complication removals (prominent over thin foramina).
- **Boundary Restriction:** Historical retrospective review reflecting practice patterns; does not provide a quantitative biomarker for hardware failure.

#### Record RES-EVD-011 (Mosbah et al. 2003)
- **Outcome Domain:** Hardware removal & morbidity
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Miniplate removal incidence and clinical justifications in trauma and orthognathic surgery.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified that $14\%$ of patients presented with localized post-surgical hardware pain; early removals were driven by infection and exposure.
- **Boundary Restriction:** Recommends against routine hardware removal in asymptomatic patients; supports symptom-driven intervention only.

#### Record RES-EVD-012 (Elegbede et al. 2018)
- **Outcome Domain:** Patient-reported trauma satisfaction
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE` (PMC6326617)
- **Claim Verified:** Psychometric reliability of FACE-Q scales in patients undergoing primary repair of facial fractures.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified in full text that all 6 FACE-Q scales demonstrated good-to-excellent reliability (Cronbach's $\alpha = 0.824\text{--}0.969$). Smoking, Le Fort fractures, and MMF predicted lower scores.
- **Boundary Restriction:** Validates individual PROM scales measuring subjective patient experience; does **not** validate an external composite formula combining radiological and clinical points.

#### Record RES-EVD-013 (Pusic et al. 2013)
- **Outcome Domain:** Psychometric appearance scoring
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`
- **Claim Verified:** Development and Rasch psychometric validation of the FACE-Q Satisfaction with Facial Appearance Scale.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified construction of a 10-item unidimensional interval scale ($0\text{ to }100$) with high person separation index ($0.88$) and Cronbach's $\alpha$ ($0.96$).
- **Boundary Restriction:** Developed in elective aesthetic populations; represents an appearance-specific PROM, not a global trauma outcome evaluation rubric.

#### Record RES-EVD-014 (Mittermiller et al. 2019)
- **Outcome Domain:** Classification & evaluation reliability
- **Assigned Status:** `VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE` (PMC6834479)
- **Claim Verified:** Multicenter observer agreement and accuracy of the comprehensive AO CMF classification system.
- **Specific Verification Note:** Verified in full text across 200 CT scans that Level 2 location variables showed higher agreement ($\kappa=0.69$, $94\%$) than Level 3 displacement morphology ($\kappa=0.59$, $91\%$).
- **Boundary Restriction:** Highlights that displacement and comminution characteristics have lower inter-observer reproducibility, cautioning against unanchored qualitative rubric rules.

### 5. Unresolved Questions & Next Evidence Gaps

1. **Absence of Multicenter Validated Millimeter Reduction Cutoffs:** While Ho et al. established baseline physiological asymmetry ($\le 1.98\text{ mm}$), no published trial has prospectively validated whether a $2.0\text{ mm}$ vs $3.0\text{ mm}$ residual step-off produces detectable aesthetic impairment across diverse trauma populations.
2. **Missing Longitudinal PROM Trajectories:** Elegbede et al. evaluated FACE-Q at 1 month postoperatively. Long-term trajectories at 6, 12, and 24 months post-repair remain sparsely documented in trauma literature.
3. **Inter-Examiner Error in Clinical Exophthalmometry:** The discrepancy between clinical Hertel measurements ($\pm 1.5\text{--}2.0\text{ mm}$ observer variability) and CT volumetry requires calibration against 3D reconstructed orbits in multi-wall fractures.
