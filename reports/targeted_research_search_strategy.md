# Targeted Clinical Research Search Strategy Report

## Maxillofacial Fracture Treatment-Outcome Evaluator Project — Phase 3

### 1. Objective and Scope Boundary
The objective of Phase 3 is to discover and verify an initial batch of original clinical studies, multicenter randomized trials, and high-quality systematic reviews that directly address the critical evidence gaps identified in Phase 2 cross-chapter textbook extraction:
- **Scope Focus:** Postoperative treatment-outcome evaluation (pre- vs. post-operative imaging restoration, clinical function, stability, safety/complications, and validated patient-reported outcomes).
- **Explicit Exclusion:** Studies limited purely to acute diagnostic screening, epidemiological incidence without treatment outcomes, animal models, or finite element models without clinical validation.

---

### 2. Research Priorities & Target Outcome Domains

1. **Priority 1: Quantitative ZMC Skeletal Alignment & Facial Symmetry**
   - *Target:* Objective 3D CT surface matching, Mean Surface Distance (MSD), Root Mean Square Distance (RMSD), Hausdorff Distance (HD95), and normal baseline midfacial variation.
2. **Priority 2: Orbital Reconstruction, Volumetry, Enophthalmos & Diplopia**
   - *Target:* Postoperative orbital volume restoration (CT volumetry $\Delta\text{mL}$), linear enophthalmos conversion ratios ($\text{mm}/\text{mL}$), Hertel exophthalmometry thresholds ($\ge 2\text{ mm}$), and cardinal gaze motility.
3. **Priority 3: Mandibular Healing, Occlusal Restoration, and Range of Motion**
   - *Target:* Maximum interincisal opening (MIO in mm), dynamic dental group function, open vs. closed condylar reduction outcomes, and IMF duration limits ($\le 2\text{ vs. } 4\text{--}6\text{ weeks}$).
4. **Priority 4: Postoperative Complications, Hardware Morbidity & Revision Surgery**
   - *Target:* Multi-year titanium plate removal incidence, specific indications (infection, cold intolerance, neurovascular palpability), and late sinonasal/lacrimal sequelae.
5. **Priority 5: Validated Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs)**
   - *Target:* Psychometric instruments validated in facial trauma (FACE-Q Craniofacial/Aesthetic modules, Derriford Appearance Scale [DAS-59/DAS-24], Mandibular Function Impairment Questionnaire [MFIQ]).
6. **Priority 6: Measurement Reliability, Reproducibility & Classification Agreement**
   - *Target:* Inter- and intra-observer reliability (Fleiss' and Cohen's $\kappa$) of CMF fracture classification and post-reduction grading (AO CMF system).

---

### 3. Search Methodology & Information Sources

- **Primary Databases Searched:** PubMed / MEDLINE, PubMed Central (PMC), Europe PMC, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library, and ScienceDirect/SpringerLink/Lippincott publisher repositories.
- **Search Period:** 1991 to 2026 (spanning seminal biomechanical/volumetric landmark papers to modern 3D CT and PROM validation studies).
- **Search Execution & Filtering:**
  - Queries combined MeSH terms, Boolean operators, and specific outcome strings.
  - Verification enforced through DOI cross-referencing and PubMed identifier (PMID) confirmation.
  - Distinctions maintained between full-text reviewed open-access articles and abstract-verified paywalled publications.

### 6. Unresolved Evidence Targets for Future Batches
1. **Direct Hertel vs. CT Volumetry Threshold Agreement:** Large-scale prospective series evaluating whether Hertel exophthalmometer inter-examiner error ($\pm 1.5\text{--}2.0\text{ mm}$) reliably matches 3D CT volume restoration in comminuted ZMC fractures.
2. **Standardized Trismus Cutoffs Post-Trauma:** Consensus criteria distinguishing normal variation from pathological post-traumatic trismus ($<35\text{ mm}$ vs. $<40\text{ mm}$).
3. **Objective Neurosensory Recovery Metrics:** Prospective trials reporting static 2-point discrimination (mm) and Semmes-Weinstein monofilament thresholds following inferior alveolar and infraorbital nerve decompression.
